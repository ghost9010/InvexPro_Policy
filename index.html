01

Overview & Architecture

This Privacy Policy applies to Invoice Pro – Stock Management Software (referred to as the "Application", "App", or "Service"), published by GHOST ("Service Provider", "we", "us", or "our").

Invoice Pro is designed as an offline-first business productivity tool. Unlike cloud-dependent management apps, Invoice Pro does not transmit or synchronize your business records to external servers. All data belongs exclusively to you and remains within your device's secure local sandbox.

03

Information & Permissions

A. Data Handled Locally

The Application processes the following data entirely on your device:

  • Shop Profile: Shop name, owner name, phone number, address, and GST number.
  • Stock Catalog: Product names, barcodes, stock counts, categories, and purchase/sale pricing.
  • Billing & Sales: Sales transactions, payment modes, invoice histories, and GST calculations.
  • App Security PIN: Optional App Lock PIN stored as a cryptographic hash (never stored in plain text).
B. Device Permissions
  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Used strictly for real-time barcode scanning and capturing product photos. Camera frames are processed locally; no video or photo streams are uploaded or transmitted.

You can grant or revoke this permission at any time through your device settings without affecting core offline stock management.

04

How Your Information Is Used

Because all data remains on your device, it is used strictly for local operations:

  • Managing stock quantities, categories, and low-stock alerts.
  • Generating GST invoices, sales summaries, and printable PDF documents.
  • Creating encrypted backup files for offline data safety.
  • Securing access through optional App Lock PIN authentication.
05

No Third-Party Tracking Services

Invoice Pro does not integrate any third-party SDKs that collect or process user data:

  • No Ad Networks: No advertisements, banners, or ad trackers.
  • No Analytics SDKs: No screen tracking, analytics beacons, or telemetry collectors.
  • No Third-Party Logins: No social login tracking or third-party authentication services.
06

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your data with any third party.

Because all records are stored locally on your device, the Service Provider has no access to your database. Sharing occurs only when you choose to export or send a PDF invoice or backup file using your phone's apps (such as WhatsApp, Gmail, etc.).

07

Storage & Security Measures

We implement industry-standard safeguards to protect data on your device:

  • Local Encryption: Database files are stored with AES-256 encryption within Android's sandboxed storage.
  • Encrypted Backups: Database exports use AES-256 encryption with optional password protection.
  • Secure Transport: Any routine network requests (such as version checks) use secure HTTPS connections exclusively.
08

Data Retention & Deletion

  • On-Device Control: Your data remains on your device as long as you choose to keep it.
  • Complete Deletion: You can permanently delete all records by clearing the app data in Android Settings > Apps > Invoice Pro > Storage > Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the application.
Google Play Deletion Policy Compliance
Because Invoice Pro does not maintain remote user databases or cloud servers, no server-side deletion request is required. Uninstalling the app permanently purges all records. For any inquiries, contact growwithghost980@gmail.com.
09

Backup & Export Controls

Invoice Pro gives you full ownership over your data:

  • Export Encrypted Backups: Save a complete backup copy of your database to any private folder on your device.
  • PDF Export: Generate and share standard GST invoices and sales reports.
10

Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Invoice Pro is a commercial inventory management application intended for business owners and merchants. It is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

11

User Rights & Controls

You have full rights to access, modify, export, or permanently erase your business records directly through the App at any time.
